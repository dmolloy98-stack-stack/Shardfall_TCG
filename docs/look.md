# The look book

How Shardfall looks and feels, so every new look fits the rest. **The owner decides the look**;
this page records those decisions and the vision behind them, in plain words. Options for a new
look must fit it (the `look-advisor` agent reads this first). If a decision here is wrong or
out of date, the owner's word wins: correct the page.

Started 24 Nov 2026 from what was already in the game and the docs. The owner hasn't reviewed it
yet, so anything not marked "owner" is Claude's reading of the game as it is.

## The feel

- **Playful on the surface, with a deep, dark story underneath** (owner). Touchstone:
  *Adventure Time*. Tone and painted glow: *Arcane* (owner pointed to both).
- **Everything is a shard.** Cards, mana, health and the turn marker are crystal shapes; the
  world shattered, and the look shows it: cracks, glass, light leaking through.
- **Magic, not machine,** on the surface. Hints of the hidden story (the break was digital, Boss
  Level's "level up") may hide inside the look, but only where a player who looks closely would
  find them, never where they would confuse play. See `docs/world.md` for the clue log.

## What the game looks like today

- **Night crystal.** Deep indigo night (`--table` `#070a18`), ice-blue rims and glows
  (`#9cc2ff`), warm brass gold for titles and key buttons (`#d9a64c`), parchment text
  (`#f2e7cf`). The colour theme moved from violet to ice-blue on 15 Nov 2026.
- **Type:** IM Fell English SC for titles (old, hand-cut letters), Alegreya Sans for everything
  else.
- **Cards** are frosted crystal panes tinted by their fracture, with lead lines and a fine crack,
  like stained glass. Rarity is a frame and a small gem.
- **Board:** Shattered Crystal by default (dark crystal slabs split by glowing seams). Other
  boards in Settings: Stained Glass, Crystal Altar, Shattered Battlefield, War Map.
- **Players:** blue (`#4cc9f0`) and pink (`#ff5d8f`). Health is a life crystal: green from 75%,
  orange down to 35%, then red and cracked, beating like a heart when low.
- **Protected** (half damage): a shell of 24 pale-blue shards on the health gem that light one by
  one (one never lights), and a band of light that fills the ring around a Protected card and
  stops at 99%, with small tick marks travelling round it.
- **Calm over busy:** the owner worries about visual clutter (24 Nov 2026). Claude's reading, for
  the owner to confirm: a new look should replace movement rather than add to it where it can.
- **Movement:** big moments get big motion (entrances scale with a card's cost, the big shard
  cracks the table). Looping effects rest when Heavy effects are off (phones start with them
  off) and when the game dozes; big movement respects "reduce motion".

## Rules every new look keeps

- Readable at phone size (the board shrinks to about 0.38 on a landscape iPhone): text, numbers
  and tap targets come first, decoration second.
- A looping effect goes on the Heavy effects list; nothing must make phones run hot.
- A clue stays subtle, and is logged in `docs/world.md` once chosen.
- Art (card pictures, champion portraits) and sound are the owner's; looks use what the game
  draws in code: colour, light, shape, layout and movement.

## Inspiration the owner has pointed to

Card games: Hearthstone, Magic: The Gathering Arena, Teamfight Tactics. Feel: Apex Legends (the
Kraber), Deadlock, Cyberpunk 2077, Borderlands, Overwatch (shields). Tone: *Arcane*, *Adventure
Time*. Not Legends of Runeterra or Gwent (not played). Ideas are for inspiration, never copies.
